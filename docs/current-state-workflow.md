# Current-State Workflow Assessment

## 1. Patient Referral and Intake

Patients enter the recruitment pipeline through five different vendors.

Recruitment workload is distributed across the intake specialists, with clinical trial sites assigned between them to balance patient volume. Each intake specialist is responsible for monitoring incoming referrals associated with their assigned sites.

The recruitment tracker is a shared Excel workbook organized by site, with each site maintaining its own tab. When a new patient enters the pipeline, the intake specialist responsible for that site adds the patient to the corresponding tracker tab and keeps that site's information updated throughout the recruitment process.  

## 2. Patient Screening

The intake specialist screens each referred patient against the trial's inclusion and exclusion criteria.

If the patient meets the eligibility criteria, the patient is referred for the next stage of the recruitment workflow.

If the patient does not meet the eligibility criteria, the intake specialist updates both the site's tracker tab and the corresponding vendor portal to document the outcome.

## 3. ROI Authorization

Patients who pass the initial eligibility screening transition to the authorization stage.

An ROI authorization document is prepared and sent to the patient electronically for signature. Up to three follow-up attempts are made when the authorization remains unsigned.

If the ROI is not signed after the third follow-up attempt, the patient is classified as lost and exits the active recruitment pipeline.

If the ROI is signed, the patient proceeds to medical-record retrieval.

## 4. Medical Record Retrieval and Secondary Eligibility Review

After authorization is obtained, relevant medical records are requested from the patient's healthcare facility or facilities.

Once the records are received, they are reviewed against the trial's inclusion and exclusion criteria to determine whether the available clinical information continues to support eligibility.

Patients who continue to meet the criteria are handed off to the Clinical Research Coordinator (CRC) for the next stage of trial evaluation.

When a patient does not meet the eligibility criteria, the outcome is manually documented across the systems used to manage the workflow, including the shared recruitment tracker, a separate medical-record retrieval tracker, the vendor portal, and the CRM.
